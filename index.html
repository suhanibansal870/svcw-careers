What is wastewater treatment?

“You ask a high schooler, do you know what happens to your water when it leaves your house? The answer is no.”

Sameet Shankar, Maintenance Director

When you flush the toilet or rinse something down the sink, the water doesn't just disappear. It travels through sewer pipes to a treatment plant. For Belmont, Redwood City, San Carlos and the communities served by West Bay Sanitary District, that plant is Silicon Valley Clean Water in Redwood City.

There, the water gets screened, cleaned by bacteria that eat the waste and tested in the lab before it goes back into the San Francisco Bay. The plant runs 24 hours a day, every day, and it takes operators, mechanics, electricians, engineers, scientists and office staff to keep it going.
